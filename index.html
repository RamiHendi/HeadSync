🔧 Setup

Get started in minutes

Follow these steps to connect HeadSync with OpenTrack on your PC.

PC OpenTrack Configuration

  • 1 Launch OpenTrack on your PC.
  • 2 Set Input to UDP over network.
  • 3 Click the Settings (hammer/wrench icon) next to the Input dropdown.
  • 4 Ensure the Port is set to 4242.
  • 5 Click OK.

Mobile HeadSync App

  • 1 Launch HeadSync and grant Camera Permissions.
  • 2 Mount your phone in Landscape orientation facing your head.
  • 3 Swipe up the Retractable Control Panel from the bottom.
  • 4 WiFi: Enter your PC's IP address in the IP Address field.
  • 5 USB (recommended): Enable USB Tethering, find the new PC IP (usually 192.168.42.x), and toggle USB Mode in the app.
  • 6 Tap START.
🎯 Operation

Calibration & Interface

Real-time visual feedback, centering, and what to expect.

📱 The Interface

  • Face Mesh Overlay — real-time visual feedback. Ensure the cyan dots align with your features.
  • FPS Monitor — displays the real-time processing speed.
  • Status Indicator — shows whether the sensor is Idle or Tracking.

🎯 Centering (CRITICAL)

For the most accurate tracking, you must calibrate the "Zero Point":

  • Look directly at your PC monitor in a natural, neutral sitting position.
  • Tap the CENTER button in the app.
✔ Done. The octopus in OpenTrack will snap to the center. HeadSync has now captured your "Reference Pose" and will calculate all movements relative to this point.
⚠ Troubleshooting

Quick fixes

Common issues and how to resolve them.

❌ Octopus is not moving

No movement in OpenTrack.

🔹 Check your PC Firewall. Ensure UDP port 4242 is allowed.
🔹 Ensure both phone and PC are on the same local network.

🔄 Tracking is Reversed

An axis moves the wrong direction.

🔹 Use the Options tab in OpenTrack to "Invert" the specific axis. The app is optimized for front-camera use.

📴 Connection drops when screen is off

Tracking stops after locking the phone.

🔹 Disable "Battery Optimization" for HeadSync in your phone's Android Settings. The app includes a WiFi-Lock, but some aggressive battery savers may override it.

📶 High latency or stutter

Tracking feels laggy.

🔹 Use USB Tethering instead of WiFi for lower latency.
🔹 Ensure no other apps are consuming heavy GPU/CPU resources on your phone.
